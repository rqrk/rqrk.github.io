Croquis - Privacy Policy

RQRK built Croquis, an app that helps urban sketchers find, capture and keep references to draw from. This privacy policy explains how Croquis handles your information.

Summary

Croquis works with your own data, on your own device and in your own iCloud account. I do not operate any servers, I do not see your data, and I do not sell or share it with anyone.

What data the app stores

The app stores the photos you capture or import, the part of each photo you keep, the notes you write, the photos of your sketches you add, and the Unsplash photos you save (their reference and photographer name, not the image files). Each photo you capture can also keep its date, place and camera details.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on your device. With Croquis Pro, it is also synced across your devices through your private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. This iCloud data belongs to you: I cannot access it.

Camera access

The app requests access to your camera to capture the scenes you want to sketch. Photos are stored only in the app, on your device and in your iCloud account.

Location

The app requests access to your location while you use it, only to save where each photo was taken so you can find your spots again on a map. Your location is stored with your photos and is never sent to me or to any third party. You can refuse it and still use the app.

Photo library

When you import a photo or add a photo of your sketch, the app uses the system photo picker: it only receives the photos you choose.

Unsplash

The Explore tab shows photos from Unsplash. To do so, the app sends your searches and filters to the Unsplash API and loads the photos from Unsplash, as any website would. When you save or share an Unsplash photo, the app reports it to Unsplash, as their guidelines require; this report contains no personal information. Unsplash handles these requests under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

Photos are only shared when you choose to, through the iOS share sheet, to the destination you pick.

Purchases

Croquis offers optional Croquis Pro subscriptions and a one-time purchase, handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. I do not collect, see, or store your payment information.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use cookies.

Account creation

The app does not require creating an account or signing in.

Deleting your data

You can delete any photo from the app at any time; it is removed from all your devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device, and you can remove the iCloud data from your iCloud settings.

Links to Other Sites

This Service contains links to other sites, such as Unsplash and photographers' profiles. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by me. I have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
