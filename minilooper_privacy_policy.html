MiniLooper - Privacy Policy

RQRK built MiniLooper, an audio looper app. This privacy policy explains how MiniLooper handles your information.

Summary

MiniLooper is built to work entirely with your own data, on your own device. I do not operate any servers, I do not see your data, and I do not sell or share it with anyone.

What data the app collects

The app stores the audio loops and recordings you create yourself. This data is created by you and never leaves your device.

Where your data is stored

All data stays locally on your device. MiniLooper does not sync your data anywhere and does not use any cloud service.

Microphone access

The app requests access to your device's microphone to record audio loops. Audio is captured and stored locally on your device only. No audio data is sent to me or to any third party.

Purchases

MiniLooper offers an optional one-time MiniLooper Pro purchase, handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. I do not collect, see, or store your payment information.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use cookies.

Account creation

The app does not require creating an account or signing in. Your recordings are stored only on your device.

Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by me. I have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-30
